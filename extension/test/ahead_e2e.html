Read-ahead end-to-end test

Plays results/ahead/005_excerpt.webm through MediaSource (appended in pieces, as YouTube does), with page.js copying the appends and ahead.js decoding, analysing and scheduling. The output is silent. Two taps record the worklet's input and output; since read-ahead mode adds no delay, output / input is the applied gain, sample by sample. A synthetic schedule (80 ms mutes every 0.5 s) gives many edges to time; the real detections are reported too.
